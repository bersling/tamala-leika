Hi, I'm Tamala.

Yes, these videos are made with AI. I don't usually like AI-generated videos either, because left to itself, AI just churns out generic slop that nobody wants to watch.

Still, when I searched YouTube for a simple explanation of implied volatility, most of what I found was so bad that I figured an AI short, with enough guidance and editing, could actually do a better job. So AI drafts the script, speaks the narration and generates the whiteboard drawings in code, while I pick the topics, spar with the machine, and keep editing or throwing out drafts until the result clears my bar.

I'm not a finance guy. These are just the concepts and market stories I learned about along the way, explained as simply as I can get the machine to explain them. None of this is financial advice.

If you like it, enjoy. If you don't, that's fine too.

## Privacy

This site sets no cookies, so there is no cookie banner. Page views are counted with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com), which doesn't use cookies or store IP addresses. The videos are hosted on YouTube and nothing loads from YouTube until you press play.
